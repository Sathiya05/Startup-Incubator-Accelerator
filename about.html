Our Story

Built to back founders who see what’s possible

LaunchForge was created for a simple purpose: to give early-stage entrepreneurs the guidance, structure and community they need to move from a promising idea to a business that can stand on its own.

Most founders do not need another slide deck — they need honest feedback, a proven process and people who have built before. That is what we organise our programs, mentorship and events around: practical support at the exact stage where it makes a difference.

We exist to shorten the distance between a promising idea and a business that can scale.

Our Approach

A founder journey in five stages

Every LaunchForge founder moves through the same disciplined path — at their own pace, with a mentor beside them. Sample stage descriptions below.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Map the problem, the customer and the assumptions hiding inside your idea.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Run customer interviews and experiments until the evidence is honest.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship the smallest product that proves value, with product mentors reviewing each cut.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Take the product to market with positioning, pricing and a first growth loop.

  5. 05

    Scale

    Systemise what works — hiring, operations and fundraising readiness.
