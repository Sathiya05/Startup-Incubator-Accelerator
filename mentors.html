Mentorship Process

How a session comes together

Five simple steps from first match to follow-through — illustrated with demo content.

  1. 1

    Select a mentor

    Browse the directory and pick the expertise that matches the challenge you are facing right now.

  2. 2

    Request a session

    Send a booking request with your preferred date and time slot — this demo stores it in your browser only.

  3. 3

    Share your goals

    Describe what you want to achieve so your mentor can prepare and arrive with the right questions.

  4. 4

    Attend the session

    Meet 1:1 for a focused conversation. Formats and times shown on this page are illustrative demos.

    Track your action items

    Leave with clear next steps and log them in the Founder Dashboard so progress stays visible.
